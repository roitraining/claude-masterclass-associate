# Play 1: Fee Pressure Radar

**Dataset:** `play1-fee-pressure.csv` (fictional)

**Goal:** A single-slide visual that shows which services are losing fee income fastest since 2023.

**Audience:** Finance committee members who skim. They should see the problem in five seconds.

**Deliverable:** One slide with a clear chart, a headline that states the finding, and a callout for the segment with the steepest decline. Optional second slide: three response options.

**Rules:** Calculate the percent decline in average fee (bps) from 2023 to 2026 per segment. Lead with the largest. Do not invent causes; label any explanation as a hypothesis.

**Constraints for all plays:** No photos or likenesses of real people. If a visual needs a person, use a text label or a simple placeholder shape. Keep supporting copy short; draft it in Word if you want a second pair of eyes. The finished asset must be safe to share outside the room.
