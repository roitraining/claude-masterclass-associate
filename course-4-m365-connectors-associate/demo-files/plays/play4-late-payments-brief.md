# Play 4: Late Payment Leaderboard

**Dataset:** `play4-late-payments.csv` (fictional)

**Goal:** A one-slide ranking of clients by money outstanding and days late, built for a collections stand-up.

**Audience:** Finance operations team and the controller.

**Deliverable:** One slide with a ranked visual, a headline with the total outstanding, and a short list of the top three follow-ups. A playful tone is fine; keep it professional.

**Rules:** Rank by amount outstanding and flag any client more than 30 days late. Use plain words, no jargon. All figures are fictional.

**Constraints for all plays:** No photos or likenesses of real people. If a visual needs a person, use a text label or a simple placeholder shape. Keep supporting copy short; draft it in Word if you want a second pair of eyes. The finished asset must be safe to share outside the room.
