# Play 3: The Forecast Bridge

**Dataset:** `play3-forecast-bridge.csv` (fictional)

**Goal:** A waterfall that explains how FY2026 revenue of 12.38 million becomes the FY2027 forecast.

**Audience:** Board members who ask what changed.

**Deliverable:** One waterfall slide with a headline stating the net change, and a footnote naming the growth assumption.

**Rules:** Verify that the opening figure plus the steps equals the closing figure, and state the result in the slide notes.

**Constraints for all plays:** No photos or likenesses of real people. If a visual needs a person, use a text label or a simple placeholder shape. Keep supporting copy short; draft it in Word if you want a second pair of eyes. The finished asset must be safe to share outside the room.
