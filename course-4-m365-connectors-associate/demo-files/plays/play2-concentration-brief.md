# Play 2: Client Concentration Heatmap

**Dataset:** `play2-client-concentration.csv` (fictional)

**Goal:** A visual that shows how much of FY2026 revenue sits with the top clients, and whether that is a risk.

**Audience:** Chief risk officer and finance committee.

**Deliverable:** One slide with a ranked visual and a headline that states the share held by the top 3 and top 5 clients. One slide with a short risk statement.

**Rules:** Compute each client's share of total revenue and the cumulative share. Use only the figures in the dataset. All client names are fictional.

**Constraints for all plays:** No photos or likenesses of real people. If a visual needs a person, use a text label or a simple placeholder shape. Keep supporting copy short; draft it in Word if you want a second pair of eyes. The finished asset must be safe to share outside the room.
