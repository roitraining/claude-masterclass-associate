**Course:** Microsoft 365 Connectors Associate

## Prerequisites

- [ ] Labs 1 and 2 completed
- [ ] Microsoft Excel, Word and PowerPoint with Claude available
- [ ] The class PowerPoint template

## Overview

Each play is a dataset and a brief. You produce a finished, shareable asset from it.

**You present to the room at the end.** The asset has to survive being looked at, so every figure on it must trace to the dataset.

---

## Part 1: Read the Brief and Compute

### Step 1: State the Goal and Audience in One Line

Read the brief for the play you choose.

**Acceptance criteria:** goal and audience written in one line.

### Step 2: Compute What the Brief Asks For

Open the dataset in Excel and ask Claude to compute it.

**Expected result:** the figures the brief requires.

### Step 3: Check Two Computed Figures by Hand

**Acceptance criteria:** two figures verified yourself.

---

## Part 2: Draft the Supporting Copy

### Step 4: Draft a Headline and Three Short Lines

Open Word and ask Claude for the supporting copy.

**Expected result:** a headline and three lines.

### Step 5: Edit the Copy to the Brief's Tone

**Acceptance criteria:** the tone matches what the brief names.

---

## Part 3: Build the Slide

### Step 6: Open a Blank Deck with the Class Template

### Step 7: Build the Slide from the Brief, the Results and Your Copy

**Expected result:** a slide, or slides, carrying one idea.

> **Troubleshooting:** if the chart does not render, build it in Excel and paste it into the slide.

### Step 8: Check Every Figure Against Excel

**Acceptance criteria:** every figure on the slide matches the workbook.

---

## Part 4: Check It Against the Brief

### Step 9: Improve Readability for the Back of the Room

> **Common Pitfall:** if the slide is crowded, ask for one idea and one chart.

### Step 10: Have Claude Check the Slide Against the Brief's Rules

**Acceptance criteria:** a list of any rules the slide breaks.

### Step 11: Fix Every Broken Rule

**Acceptance criteria:** no rule from the brief is left broken.

> **Common Pitfall:** if Claude invented a cause for a trend, label it a hypothesis or remove it.

> **Common Pitfall:** if Claude added a stock photo of a person, replace it with a text label or a plain shape.

---

## Part 5: Add Speaker Notes and Present

### Step 12: Add Three Speaker-Note Bullets

### Step 13: Present the Asset in Three Minutes

**Acceptance criteria:** the asset is presented, and you can name any discrepancy you found in the data.

## Troubleshooting Reference

| Issue | Symptom | Solution |
|---|---|---|
| Chart will not render | No chart appears on the slide | Build it in Excel and paste it into the slide |
| Invented cause | A trend is explained with no evidence | Label it a hypothesis or remove it |
| Stock photo of a person | A likeness appears on the asset | Replace it with a text label or a plain shape |
| Slide crowded | Too much on one slide | Ask for one idea and one chart |
| Numbers do not match | Play 3 disagrees with the workbook | Intended. Say so on the slide |
