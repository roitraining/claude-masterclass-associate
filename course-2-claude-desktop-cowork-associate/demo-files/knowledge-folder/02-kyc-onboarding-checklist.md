# KYC Onboarding Checklist

A new individual member must provide:
1. One government photo ID, not expired.
2. Proof of address dated within 90 days (utility bill, lease, or bank statement).
3. Taxpayer identification number.
4. Source of funds statement if the opening deposit is $10,000 or more.

## Escalation
- A name match on the sanctions screening list: stop, do not tell the member, and notify Compliance the same day.
- A mismatch between the ID and the application: pause onboarding and notify the branch manager.

## Records
Copies of the ID are kept for 7 years after the account closes.
