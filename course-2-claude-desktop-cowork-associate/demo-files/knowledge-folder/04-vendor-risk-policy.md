# Vendor Risk Policy

## Tiers
- Tier 1 (critical): handles member data or supports core processing. Full review before contract, annual review after.
- Tier 2 (important): no member data, but an outage affects operations. Review before contract, review every two years.
- Tier 3 (low): everything else. Basic checklist only.

## Required for Tier 1
Current SOC 2 Type II report, written incident notice within 48 hours, and a right-to-audit clause.

## Quotes
Any purchase above $25,000 needs at least three written quotes. The quotes must cover the same scope.
