# Wire Transfer Procedure

## Limits
- Domestic wire, online banking: $25,000 per day per member.
- Domestic wire, in branch: $100,000 per day per member. Above $100,000 needs a branch manager second signature.
- International wire: $50,000 per day per member, in branch only.

## Callback rule
Any wire above $10,000 to a new beneficiary needs a callback to the member at the phone number on file, not a number given in the request.

## Cutoff times
- Domestic wires submitted before 3:00 p.m. Eastern go out the same business day.
- International wires submitted before 1:00 p.m. Eastern go out the same business day.

## Fees
See 06-fee-schedule.csv. Fees are never waived by a teller. A branch manager may waive one wire fee per member per year.

## Recall
A wire recall request must be filed within 24 hours. Recall is not guaranteed.
