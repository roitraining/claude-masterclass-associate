# Source B: Regulator Bulletin

Publisher: Federal Credit Union Supervision Office (fictional regulator)
Title: Quarterly Deposit Rate Bulletin, Q2 2026
Published: August 20, 2026

## Method

Computed from quarterly call report filings submitted by all 4,180 reporting credit unions. Rates are the average interest paid on regular savings balances, weighted by balance, so larger institutions count for more.

## Finding

The balance-weighted average rate on regular savings was **0.48 percent** for the quarter ended June 30, 2026.

## Note

The bulletin states that unweighted averages across institutions are typically higher, because smaller institutions tend to pay more on small balances.
