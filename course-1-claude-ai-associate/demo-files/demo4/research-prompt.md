# Demo 4: Research Prompts

## Live Research prompt (instructor, web search on)

```
Research this business question for a credit union product team: What savings rates are community financial institutions in the United States currently paying on regular savings accounts, and how do published sources differ on the average? Use at least five sources. Cite every figure. Where two sources disagree, say so and explain the likely reason.
```
