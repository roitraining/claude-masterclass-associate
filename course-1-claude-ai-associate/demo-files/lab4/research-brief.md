# Lab 4: Research Deep Dive (Take-Home)

Pick one business question. Use the scenario that matches your work.

- **Financial services:** How are credit unions and community banks using generative AI for member service, and what supervisory expectations apply?
- **Healthcare:** How are hospitals using generative AI for administrative work such as scheduling and billing, and what privacy expectations apply?
- **Government:** How are local governments using generative AI for resident service requests, and what public records and transparency expectations apply?

## Deliverables

1. `write-up.md`: a report of two pages or fewer, built from `write-up-template.md`.
2. `source-log.csv`: one row for every source you opened, built from `source-log-template.csv`. Mark each kept or dropped, with a reason.
3. A short note on one place two sources disagreed and how you resolved it.
4. Your answers on `self-review-checklist.md`.

## Method

Turn on web search, start Research from the plus menu, and give Claude the question, the audience, and a requirement to cite every figure. Do not accept the first report as final.
