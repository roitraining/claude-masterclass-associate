# Self-Review Checklist

Answer yes or no for each.

- [ ] I opened at least five cited links and each one supports the claim attached to it.
- [ ] Every figure in the report has a source and a publication date.
- [ ] I dropped at least one source and wrote down why.
- [ ] Where two sources disagreed, I checked method, date and definition before choosing.
- [ ] The report says what could not be confirmed.
- [ ] I found no citation that points to a page that does not say what the report says.
- [ ] A colleague could repeat my search from the log.
