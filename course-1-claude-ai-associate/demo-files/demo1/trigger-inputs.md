# Demo 1: Trigger Test Inputs

Paste each input into a new chat. The first three should trigger the Skill. The last three should not.

## Should trigger

**T1**

Notes from my call with Ms. Okafor this morning. She wants to refinance her auto loan. I said I would send her the rate sheet by Friday. She will check with her spouse. Her question about the early payoff fee needs someone from lending to answer. Can you write up the recap?

**T2**

Minutes from the member advisory meeting: attendees were Dana (chair), Luis, Priya. We agreed to move the next meeting to the 14th. Luis will draft the survey questions. Priya will book the room. Please pull out the action items.

**T3**

Turn these bullets into a meeting summary:
- Reviewed Q3 service requests, fees are the top category
- Agreed to add a fee explainer to the website
- Marcus to own the draft, no date yet

## Should not trigger

**N1**

Summarize this in three sentences: Many credit unions are raising certificate rates this quarter as deposit competition increases, while some are holding savings rates flat to protect margins.

**N2**

Write a short email to a member explaining that our monthly maintenance fee is waived with a $500 direct deposit.

**N3**

What is the cutoff time for outgoing wires?

## What to look for

A triggered Skill produces the heading "Pinecrest Recap" followed by Summary, Decisions, Action items and Follow-up flags. An input that did not trigger the Skill produces ordinary chat output without that structure.
