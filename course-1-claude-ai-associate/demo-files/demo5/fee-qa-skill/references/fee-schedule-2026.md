# Pinecrest Credit Union Fee Schedule, effective January 1, 2026

All figures are fictional.

## Section 1: Checking

- 1.1 Basic Checking monthly maintenance fee: $8. Waived if the member has direct deposits of $500 or more in the month, OR an average daily balance of $1,500 or more.
- 1.2 Overdraft fee: $27 per item. No more than 3 overdraft fees are charged in one day.
- 1.3 Paper statement fee: $3 per month. Waived for members age 62 or older.

## Section 2: Wires

- 2.1 Outgoing domestic wire: $25.
- 2.2 Outgoing international wire: $45.
- 2.3 Incoming wire: $12.
- 2.4 Outgoing wire cutoff: 3:00 p.m. Eastern.

## Section 3: Cards

- 3.1 Replacement debit card: $10. Rush delivery adds $30.
- 3.2 Foreign transaction fee: 1.0 percent of the transaction amount.

## Section 4: Savings

- 4.1 Excess withdrawal fee: $5 for each withdrawal beyond 6 in a calendar month.
- 4.2 Early closure fee: $25 if the account is closed within 90 days of opening.
