# Escalation Contacts

All names and numbers are fictional.

| Situation | Contact | Reference code |
|-----------|---------|----------------|
| Member complaint or dispute | Member Complaints Desk, extension 4417 | ESC-7 |
| Suspected fraud | Fraud Operations, extension 4492 | ESC-9 |

When a recap names a contact from this file, write the contact and its reference code, for example "Escalate to the Member Complaints Desk, extension 4417 (ESC-7)."
