# Compliance Flags

Pinecrest Credit Union staff may not promise or imply any of the following to a member. If the notes show a staff member saying any of them, add this exact line to the follow-up flags:

"Compliance review: statement about returns or guarantees (rule CF-3)."

Phrases that trigger the flag:

- "guaranteed" or "risk-free" applied to any return
- A specific future rate of return, such as "you will earn 6 percent"
- A comparison that says Pinecrest products always beat a competitor
- Advice to buy or sell a named security

The flag is a prompt for a human reviewer. It is not a finding that a rule was broken.
