## Part 1: Choose the Question and Run Research

### Step 1: Choose Your Business Question

Pick one from `research-brief.md` or write your own.

**Expected result:** a single, answerable question.

### Step 2: Turn On Web Search and Start Research

Start Research from the plus menu.

> **Troubleshooting:** if Research cannot start, check that web search is on and that you are on a paid plan.

### Step 3: Give Claude the Question, the Audience and the Citation Rule

State the rule to cite every figure.

**Expected result:** a report with inline citations.

---

## Part 2: Verify the Sources

### Step 4: Open at Least Five Inline Citations

**Acceptance criteria:** five or more citations opened and read, not just counted.

> **Common Pitfall:** if a citation does not support the claim attached to it, drop the claim or the source, and log it.

### Step 5: Log Every Source You Opened

Record date, publisher kind, method and reliability in `source-log-template.csv`.

**Acceptance criteria:** every opened source logged with all four fields.

### Step 6: Mark Each Source Kept or Dropped

**Acceptance criteria:** at least one source dropped, with a concrete reason such as "no method" or "stale".

> **Key Insight:** a log where every source is kept is not a log. It means you did not judge them.

### Step 7: Find One Disagreement and Resolve It

Find one place two sources disagree. Check method, date and definition, then write down how you resolved it.

**Acceptance criteria:** a disagreement note comparing method and definition.

> **Troubleshooting:** if all your sources agree, search for a second definition of the same figure. Agreement often means one definition.

---

## Part 3: Revise and Write Up

### Step 8: Revise Using Only Kept Sources

Ask Claude to revise the report using only the sources you kept.

**Expected result:** a report with no dropped source behind any claim.

### Step 9: Complete the Write-Up

Fill in `write-up-template.md` with the revised answer, findings, disagreement note, and what you could not confirm.

**Acceptance criteria:** a report of two pages or fewer, with a "could not confirm" section that is not empty by default.

> **Troubleshooting:** if the report is too long, ask for the three findings that matter most.

## Troubleshooting Reference

| Issue | Symptom | Solution |
|---|---|---|
| Research will not start | Option unavailable or errors | Check that web search is on and that you are on a paid plan |
| Citation does not match | A source does not support its claim | Drop the claim or the source, and log it |
| Everything agrees | No disagreement to analyze | Search for a second definition of the same figure |
| Report too long | Over two pages | Ask for the three findings that matter most |
| Allowance running down | Usage climbing | Run Research once and revise in a normal chat |
